# S22: Background workers across `/clear` keep their lease and are re-bound

- **Assumption / source:** S22
- **Mode:** interactive
- **Design:** DESIGN §14 (live smoke list)

## Purpose

Background workers across `/clear` keep their lease and are re-bound.

## Steps

1. Interactive session; spawn a background `cadre:worker` that sleeps 60 s; run `/clear`.
2. `bd show <task>` still shows the lease; `/cadre status` still lists the agent; its notification arrives.

## Expected

Lease and binding survive; rebind only when the lease is expired.

## M0 status

PENDING-HUMAN. ADR 0001: needs interactive `/clear`.
