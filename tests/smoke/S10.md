# S10: `$.state` board survives `/reload-plugins` and a worker respawn; `$.store` rehydrates after restart

- **Assumption / source:** WE S10; §8
- **Mode:** interactive
- **Design:** DESIGN §14 (live smoke list)

## Purpose

`$.state` board survives `/reload-plugins` and a worker respawn; `$.store` rehydrates after restart.

## Steps

1. Interactive: `claude --plugin-dir ./cadre`, run a plan to `active`, `/reload-plugins`, then `/cadre status`.
2. Quit, `claude --continue`, `/cadre status`: board comes back via `$.store cadre:<repoKey>:board` and the resume card.

## Expected

Board identical after reload; after restart the one-time resume card appears and `drive` is off.
