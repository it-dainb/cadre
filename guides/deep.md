## Before step 2: talk it through
The user wants to talk the goal through first. Call explore ONCE asking what in the project the goal touches; end your turn and wait for its answer. Then, in rounds (at most {maxRounds}): score outcome, scope, acceptance and constraints from 0 to 1 (ask `{"clarity": {…}}`), show the user the scores in one line, and ask ONE question about the weakest point its answer names (AskUserQuestion, options informed by what the explorer found). Stop when the answer says the goal is clear, when the rounds are used up, or when the user says "enough". Then do step 2 with what you learned.
